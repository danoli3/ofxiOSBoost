# Boost 1.65.0 Context validation

Validation date: 2026-08-10

The consolidated iOS application passed on:

- arm64 iOS Simulator;
- physical arm64 iOS device.

Runtime coverage on both targets:

- Boost.Filesystem and Boost.Regex packaged-library linkage;
- Boost.Context `make_fcontext` plus two `jump_fcontext` stack round-trips,
  including stack-local canary preservation;
- Boost.Coroutine2 pull/push yield and resume with values `21` and `42`.

Result on both targets: `ALL TESTS PASSED`.

The release archive includes `make_fcontext`, `jump_fcontext`, and
`ontop_fcontext`. The first two were exercised at runtime; `ontop_fcontext` is
checked for binary presence but does not yet have a dedicated runtime test.

Boost.Fiber and deprecated Boost.Coroutine are separate compiled libraries.
They were not part of this validation and remain excluded.
